# Governance

The maintainers govern repository state; they do not vote mathematical claims
true.

Four decisions remain separate:

1. Accept a contribution bundle as well-formed evidence.
2. Add a result to a problem's searchable catalog.
3. Admit a mathematical claim after its external verification gate.
4. Activate an engine or prompt version.

Only designated problem maintainers may update a problem's canonical memory.
Only engine maintainers may update engine-wide process learnings. Every
promotion must cite exact evidence and preserve superseded history.

Conflicts of interest, contributor identity disputes, and prize-credit matters
must be disclosed. Repository maintainers do not speak for the Clay
Mathematics Institute or any journal, university, or prize committee.
