# Target: Birch and Swinnerton–Dyer Conjecture

- **Problem ID:** `birch-swinnerton-dyer`
- **Status:** open
- **Official source:** https://www.claymath.org/millennium/birch-and-swinnerton-dyer-conjecture/
- **Official description author:** Andrew Wiles

## Target map

The official problem concerns the relationship between the arithmetic rank of
an elliptic curve over the rationals and the behavior of its associated
L-function at `s = 1`, including the full official formulation and conditions.

A terminal solution must prove or refute the exact official statement, not a
finite computation, a special family, a probabilistic heuristic, or only one
direction under additional hypotheses.

## Campaign boundary

Every campaign must name the precise curve class, analytic continuation and
functional-equation inputs it assumes, the exact rank/order-of-vanishing
statement pursued, and its first open implication. Imported modularity,
descent, finiteness, and L-function results require primary-source checks.
