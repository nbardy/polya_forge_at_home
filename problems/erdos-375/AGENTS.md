# Erdős 375 research rules

Follow the repository `AGENTS.md`. Attack the exact counterexample endpoint in
`target.md`.

Do not search below the registry's reported \(n\leq 1.9\times10^{10}\)
verification boundary except to validate an implementation. For any candidate,
factor every interval member exactly and construct the complete prime-divisor
incidence graph. Require a verifier implementation independent of the builder
to validate either an explicit Hall obstruction or a maximum-matching
certificate.

Label all bounded negative searches as computational evidence. They do not
prove the universal conjecture. Do not claim novelty until the original
sources, Laishram--Shorey boundary, and later literature have been checked by a
human.
