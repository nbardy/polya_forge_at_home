# Erdős 617 research rules

Follow the repository `AGENTS.md`. Attack the exact \(r=5\) counterexample
endpoint in `target.md`.

Use symmetry breaking only when it is proved satisfiability-preserving.
Preserve the decoded coloring, not merely a solver's internal model. Every
candidate must be checked over all 230230 induced six-vertex subsets by a
verifier implementation independent of the builder.

Label solver timeouts and unsuccessful searches as computational evidence.
Do not infer the universal conjecture from failure to find a coloring. Do not
claim novelty until the original Erdős--Gyárfás sources and later literature
have been checked by a human.
