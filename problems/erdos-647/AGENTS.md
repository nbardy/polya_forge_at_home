# Erdős 647 research rules

Follow the repository `AGENTS.md`. Attack the exact unconditional existential
statement in `target.md`.

For every proposed witness, check all integers `1 <= m < n`; checking only a
short interval below `n` is invalid. Compute divisor counts by exact
factorization or an exact sieve, preserve equality cases, and require a
verifier implementation independent of the builder's implementation.

Label finite negative searches as computational evidence only. Do not turn a
search cutoff, pruning lemma, conditional theorem, or source audit into the
research objective or completion criterion. Do not claim novelty or resolution
until primary-source fidelity and external human review are complete.
