# Target: Hodge Conjecture

- **Problem ID:** `hodge-conjecture`
- **Status:** open
- **Official source:** https://www.claymath.org/millennium/hodge-conjecture/
- **Official description author:** Pierre Deligne

## Target map

The official conjecture asks whether the specified rational Hodge classes on
smooth projective complex algebraic varieties are rational linear combinations
of classes of algebraic cycles, with the exact conventions and scope fixed by
the official description.

A terminal solution must establish or refute that exact universal statement.
Proofs for restricted dimensions, codimensions, varieties, coefficients, or
stronger auxiliary hypotheses are partial results unless they close the full
official endpoint.

## Campaign boundary

Every campaign must state the variety class, smoothness/projectivity/base
field, cohomology and coefficient conventions, cycle equivalence, codimension,
and the exact comparison map. Do not conflate the integral and rational Hodge
conjectures.

This summary is not the prize statement. Freeze claims against the official
description linked above.
