# Target: Navier–Stokes Existence and Smoothness

- **Problem ID:** `navier-stokes`
- **Status:** open
- **Official source:** https://www.claymath.org/millennium/navier-stokes-equation/
- **Official description author:** Charles L. Fefferman

## Target map

The official problem asks for one of the specified global regularity/existence
conclusions or one of the specified breakdown alternatives for three-
dimensional incompressible Navier–Stokes, with the domains, data, forcing,
smoothness, energy, and periodicity/decay conditions fixed in the official
description.

A terminal result must provide a complete proof or counterexample satisfying
one official alternative. Numerical blow-up, conditional criteria, simplified
models, or a priori estimates that assume the missing regularity are partial
evidence only.

## Campaign boundary

Every campaign must state the domain, solution concept, pressure treatment,
initial data, forcing, boundary/decay conditions, scaling, and literal first
open implication toward an official alternative. Energy and sign checks are
mandatory.

This summary is not the prize statement. Freeze claims against the official
description linked above.
