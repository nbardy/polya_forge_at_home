# Target: Riemann Hypothesis

- **Problem ID:** `riemann-hypothesis`
- **Status:** open
- **Official source:** https://www.claymath.org/millennium/riemann-hypothesis/
- **Official description author:** Enrico Bombieri

## Target map

The official hypothesis states that every nontrivial zero of the analytically
continued Riemann zeta function has real part `1/2`, with the exact conventions
and equivalent endpoint formulations described by the official source.

A terminal result must prove the universal statement or exhibit and rigorously
certify a nontrivial zero off the critical line. Finite zero verification,
density estimates, analogues for other zeta functions, probabilistic models,
or consequences conditional on RH are partial results only.

## Campaign boundary

Every campaign must state the function, continuation and functional-equation
inputs, zero multiplicity/convention, domain, explicit estimates, and exact
universal quantifiers. Numerical zero claims require certified error bounds.

This summary is not the prize statement. Freeze claims against the official
description linked above.
