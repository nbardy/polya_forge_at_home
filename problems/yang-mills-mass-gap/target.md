# Target: Yang–Mills Existence and Mass Gap

- **Problem ID:** `yang-mills-mass-gap`
- **Status:** open
- **Official source:** https://www.claymath.org/millennium/yang-mills-the-maths-gap/
- **Official description authors:** Arthur Jaffe and Edward Witten

## Target map

The official problem asks for a rigorous construction of quantum Yang–Mills
theory on four-dimensional Euclidean space for every compact simple gauge
group, satisfying the required axiomatic properties and possessing a positive
mass gap, with the exact statement fixed by the official description.

A terminal result must supply that construction and gap proof at the official
generality. Classical Yang–Mills estimates, lattice computations, perturbative
expansions, lower-dimensional models, or physics arguments without the
required constructive and axiomatic control are partial evidence only.

## Campaign boundary

Every campaign must state dimension, signature, gauge group, regularization,
continuum limit, observable/state space, axioms, and exact spectral definition
of the mass gap. Renormalization and gauge-fixing assumptions must be explicit.

This summary is not the prize statement. Freeze claims against the official
description linked above.
